Recommended test sequence
1. Refresh Health and confirm market/execution are healthy. 2. Send one manual entry. 3. Inspect the Decision and Execution boxes. 4. Watch Timeline and Overview for protection placement, supervision state, and health events. 5. Run Reconcile. 6. Close via Supervise or opposite-side flip and confirm the close attribution is persisted.
